# Population-specific eQTL effects: chromosome 22

**351 SNP–gene pairs** show a significant EUR–YRI effect difference after Bonferroni correction across **1,137,398 interaction tests** (p ≤ 4.396e-08), involving **23 genes**. The analysis uses 358 EUR and 87 YRI individuals, shared common SNPs, and HC3 robust standard errors. These are exploratory differences in association, not proven population-specific causal effects; no PCA adjustment is included.
